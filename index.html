research

CARVE — continual adaptation through the reuse of validated expertise

chip macro placement · continual adaptation · specialist reuse · local validation

Developed a framework for adapting pretrained macro-placement policies to new circuits when the provider’s training data are unavailable. CARVE keeps a frozen base policy and unchanged specialists, authorizes each specialist only for tasks on which it passes local validation, and trains a new specialist from the frozen base only when no saved one qualifies. The paper gives expected-performance guarantees for repeated reuse and matching bounds on the local samples needed for reliable reuse. In macro placement, a reuse-first variant cut recorded training time by 58.5% (9.66 → 4.01 h) while mean HPWL gain moved only from 8.41% to 7.86%, and a simulated receiving operator reused imported specialists on six of seven new IBM circuits without training of its own.

[arXiv]

SPHERE — spatial coherent risk control for replay

online continual learning · representation geometry · experience replay

Co-developed a replay-allocation method for online continual learning. SPHERE uses a representation kernel to aggregate signed prospective loss changes, damping isolated spikes while keeping coherent increases, and then allocates replay through entropy-regularized transport blended with uniform rehearsal. Experiments cover noisy-label vision tasks, continual instruction tuning of language models, and code-generation reinforcement learning with incomplete test rewards.

[arXiv]

SASST — selection-aware stress testing for interactive agents

interactive agents · discovery / confirmation · semantic task reweighting

A protocol for checking whether a selected agent workflow’s apparent advantage survives independent confirmation and changes in task composition. Semantic stress rules are learned from pre-execution features on discovery tasks, then frozen and evaluated on separate confirmation tasks. Support, stability, and joint-inference checks allow an explicit no-claim outcome when the evidence is insufficient.

[arXiv]

PSS-Social — safe robot navigation in dense crowds

deep RL · density generalization · robot navigation

Developed and benchmarked a reinforcement-learning approach to dense-crowd navigation, combining density-invariant observations, density-randomized training, and physics-informed proxemic reward shaping. The study evaluates zero-shot transfer to larger crowds and separates goal-reaching, collision-free success, and freezing failures across learned and analytical baselines.

[arXiv][code]

SIREN — selection-aware inference for adaptive LLM evaluation NeurIPS 2026

post-selection inference · held-out evaluation · LLM benchmarking

Co-developed a repeated-split reporting protocol for the fresh-data performance of tune-then-deploy LLM procedures under explicit tuning budgets. It freezes a post-search shortlist, separates selection from held-out evaluation, and quantifies uncertainty with an item-level multiplier bootstrap. Led MMLU-Pro tuning experiments with vLLM-served models and DSPy prompt optimization.

[arXiv]

Core-Halo — decentralizing large-scale fixed-point problems

multi-agent learning · local read access · decentralized evaluation

Co-developed a framework that separates disjoint update ownership (core) from overlapping read-only context (halo), preserving the dependencies needed to recover the original fixed point under a locality condition. Built SUMO/PettingZoo traffic-control benchmarks and contributed PageRank and smart-grid experiments comparing decentralized and centralized solutions.

[arXiv]
